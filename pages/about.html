About Me

Hi, I'm Carlos, an IT professional with over 20 years of experience and a background working with Skyward systems in education.

I created SkyChat as a personal project to explore how AI can make Skyward-related information easier to find and understand.

The idea is simple: ask a question and get a clear answer from the available documentation.

SkyChat is an independent project and is still a work in progress as I continue learning and improving it.

Thanks for checking it out!

— Carlos
