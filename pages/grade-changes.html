Grade Changes After Posting

Follow these steps to correct a grade after grades have already been posted.

Steps

  1. Open Grade Posting Status.
  2. Select the grading period you want to change.
  3. Highlight the appropriate class.
  4. Click Request Grade Changes.
  5. Enter the reason for the grade change.
  6. Click Yes to continue.
  7. Make the necessary grade changes.
  8. Click Save.
  9. Click Complete Grade Changes when finished.
Important: Verify the correct class and grading period before making any changes.
